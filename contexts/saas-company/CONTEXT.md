# SaaS Company Simulation — Ubiquitous Language

## Company and people

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Company | The software business operating one or more SaaS products. |
| Team | A stable functional group: Leadership, Product, Engineering, Design, QA, Customer Success, Sales, or Finance. |
| Member | A person assigned to one primary Team and one or more Products. |
| Role | A Member's responsibility and access level; it is not the same as a job title. |
| Squad | A temporary cross-functional delivery group for a Product or major Initiative. |

## Product delivery

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Product | A SaaS offering owned by the Company and used by Customers. |
| Initiative | A measurable business outcome that may span multiple Releases. |
| Roadmap Item | A planned product outcome, ordered by priority; it is not a promise of delivery. |
| Sprint | A fixed delivery cycle used by a Squad to complete a selected Sprint Goal. |
| Work Item | A unit of delivery: Epic, Story, Task, Bug, or Support Request. |
| Release | A verified version or feature set made available to Customers. |
| Quality Gate | The required checks before a Release may be deployed: review, test, QA approval, and release approval. |

## Customers and commercial work

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Lead | A prospective customer that has not yet entered a commercial agreement. |
| Customer | An organization with an active agreement for a Product. |
| Workspace | A Customer's isolated SaaS environment, including its Users and data. |
| Subscription | The commercial entitlement that grants a Workspace access to a Product plan. |
| Support Request | A customer-reported question, incident, or change request. |

## Retail sales recording

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Merchant | A person or organization that operates a shop and uses the Product to record sales. A Merchant may become a Customer after entering an agreement. |
| Shop | A Merchant's physical or online point of sale. A Workspace may contain one or more Shops only if that need is confirmed. |
| Sale | A completed transaction that records money received by a Shop. A Sale is not an Invoice. |
| Daily Revenue | The total money received from recorded Sales during a Shop's defined business day. |

## Operating states

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Ready | A Work Item has clear scope, acceptance criteria, owner, and priority. |
| In Progress | The assigned Team is actively working on the Work Item. |
| In Review | A Work Item awaits peer, product, or QA review. |
| Blocked | Work cannot continue without an identified dependency or decision. |
| Done | Work meets its acceptance criteria; it is not necessarily released. |
| Released | A verified Work Item is available to the intended Customers. |
